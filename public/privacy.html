Privacy Policy

How NEWS-MONSTER ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information.

LAST UPDATED: AUGUST 2026

1. Overview

NEWS-MONSTER is an automated news video network. It ingests publicly available news headlines and renders them into AI-generated video shorts that are published to public video platforms such as YouTube. This policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and the choices you have.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the minimum amount of information needed to operate the service:

  • Public news headlines — fetched from licensed/public news APIs (NewsAPI, NewsData.io).
  • Automated usage data — non-identifying server logs and error metrics (IP address, request timestamps, error codes) used solely for reliability and security.
  • LinkedIn data (only if you connect the app) — when you authorize this app on LinkedIn, we access only the profile fields required to function. We do not post on your behalf and do not access your message inbox or connections.
  • Cookies — our landing page is a static site and does not itself set tracking or advertising cookies.

3. How We Use Information

  • To generate, render, and publish automated news videos.
  • To associate your LinkedIn Page with the app and facilitate social publishing.
  • To monitor service health, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. AI Content Disclosure

Video content is generated by automated systems. Output is labeled through platform disclosure where available. News content links back to the original source for attribution.

5. Third-Party Services

The app relies on third-party services whose own privacy policies apply to the data they process: Google (YouTube), LinkedIn, News APIs, and hosting providers. We encourage you to review their policies.

6. Data Retention

Logs Used for debugging are retained for a limited period (up to 30 days) and then deleted. Published videos are retained to be publicly available on the platforms they were distributed to and can be removed on request.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction, or deletion of personal data we hold about you, and may revoke the app's social authorization at any time from your LinkedIn settings. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

8. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards to protect data, including encryption in transit and limited access to systems. No method of transmission is 100% secure — we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the service evolves. The "Last Updated" date will be updated accordingly. Material changes will be noted by re-publishing this page.
