# Content Strategy

## Brand Identity

**Name**: NEWS-MONSTER  
**Tagline**: "Unfiltered Breaking News From The Future"  
**Logo**: NM gold emblem (no icon)  
**Personality**: Authoritative, fast-paced, mysterious, exclusive

## Channel Pillars

1. **Breaking News**: First-to-publish on major tech stories
2. **Deep Analysis**: Why stories matter, hidden implications
3. **Future Gazing**: Trends, predictions, emerging tech
4. **Exclusive Reveals**: Insider perspectives on industry moves

## Coverage Categories (13)

Primary: technology, ai, science, cybersecurity  
Secondary: gaming, robotics, space, quantum, biotech  
Tertiary: programming, sports, politics, lifestyle

## Brand Voice Guidelines

### Tone
- Authoritative but accessible
- Fast-paced but clear
- Mysterious but trustworthy
- Exclusive but transparent

### Hook Formulas
```
1. "Why [Company] Buried This Secret For Years"
2. "Nobody Expected What [Product] Just Did"
3. "The Hidden Feature [Company] Never Told You About"
4. "[Number] Years Later, We Found The Truth"
5. "What [Company] Doesn't Want You To Know"
```

### Narration Rules
- 8-15 words per sentence
- Active voice preferred
- Present tense for breaking news
- Suspense-building structure: set up → reveal → impact
- End scenes with hooks that drive to next scene

### Narrative Arc
1. **Hook** (0-3s): Create urgency/curiosity, max 10 words
2. **Context** (3-8s): What happened, who, where
3. **Reveal** (8-15s): The hidden detail or unexpected angle
4. **Impact** (15-25s): Why it matters, implications
5. **Close** (25-30s): CTA, follow prompt

### CTA Format
```
"Follow NEWS-MONSTER for [value proposition] 
you won't find anywhere else."
```

## Content Quality Standards

- Headline: 20-120 characters
- Hook: <10 words, creates urgency
- Narration: 8-15 words per sentence
- Duration: 25-35s total
- Scenes: 5-8 per video
- Visual prompts: Include "cinematic, 8k, vertical 9:16" in every prompt
