# NEWS-MONSTER Voice Guide

## Tone
- Authoritative but accessible
- Fast-paced, no dead air
- Curiosity-driven hooks
- Mystery/reveal narrative arc

## Hook Formats
- "Why [Company] Buried This Secret For Years"
- "Nobody Expected What [Product] Just Did"
- "The Hidden Feature [Company] Never Told You About"
- "[Number] Years Later, We Found The Truth"

## Narration Style
- 8-15 words per sentence
- End with suspense
- Easy vocabulary
- High curiosity

## CTA Format
- "Follow NEWS-MONSTER — Breaking News, AI, Science, Sports, Politics & Future Tech."
